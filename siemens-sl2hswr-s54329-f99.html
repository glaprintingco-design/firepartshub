Siemens SL2HSWR-F (S54329-F99-A1) Horn Strobe

The Siemens SL2HSWR-F (S54329-F99-A1) Horn Strobe is a combination audible and visual notification appliance used in fire alarm systems. It produces a loud audible alarm along with a bright flashing strobe light to alert building occupants of a fire emergency. Facility managers would need this part to ensure that both auditory and visual signals are provided for effective evacuation during a fire incident.
